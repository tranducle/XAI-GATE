## Manuscript Rule

The manuscript may now report the no-training XAI-SurfaceBench results, all-regime boundary table, tuned-baseline envelope, tuned-XAI-Gate envelope with fixed-vs-expanded exposure distinction, KDDCup99 calibrated anchor, UNSW-NB15 public test-split calibration anchor, de-duplicated UNSW rerun, TON_IoT calibrated anchor, paired statistical follow-up, action-profile provenance, and action-profile sensitivity, but only with the tradeoff and limitation language recorded above. Stronger deployment claims still require broader deployment traces, target edge/gateway hardware measurements, or executed SME/operator evidence.
